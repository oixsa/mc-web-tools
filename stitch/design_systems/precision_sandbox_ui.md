## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-utility, modern web application suite tailored specifically for Minecraft builders, technical architects, and community creators. Rooted in utility minimalism with tactile digital nuances, the interface departs from clunky game-mimicking skeumorphism in favor of a fast, crisp, browser-grade workspace.

The brand persona is precise, empowering, modern, and delightfully focused. Visual complexity is minimized so the user’s voxel plans, schematics, and color gradients take center stage. Key characteristics include:
- **Clean modular card structures:** Defined borders, subtle depth separation, and crisp content containment.
- **Vibrant interactive feedback:** Electric indigo primary accents coupled with an energetic emerald/teal indicator set for badges, tags, active states, and focus halos.
- **Game-adjacent utility:** High density, rapid visual parsing, and monospaced or ultra-legible numeric labeling where block coordinates, dimensions, and tier recipes require absolute clarity.

## Colors

The palette balances cool slate structural neutrals with an electric indigo primary brand tone (`#5465FF`) and a high-visibility emerald teal secondary accent (`#06D6A0`).

- **Primary (`#5465FF`):** Powers focal actions, active utility buttons, primary card links, and focus rings. Lightened shifts appear on hover (`#4454EE`), with a low-opacity wash (`#5465FF14`) reserved for active filter chips.
- **Secondary / Utility Accent (`#06D6A0`):** Acts as the functional beacon across tool states, 'New'/'Beta' indicators, live calculation states, and successful schematic exports.
- **Neutral Palette (`#111827`, `#5A6B82`, `#F4F7FC`):** Base canvas sits on `#F4F7FC`, establishing clear contrast with pure white card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`). Text adopts an ink-like slate tone (`#111827`) for high contrast readability under diverse monitor calibrations.
- **Borders & Separators:** Low-contrast architectural outlines (`#E5EAF2`) keep the interface structured without visually overpowering densely packed tool cards.
