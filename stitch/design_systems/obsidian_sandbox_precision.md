## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, dark-mode-first environment crafted for Minecraft architects, schematic technicians, and high-level sandbox builders. Blending the depth of an obsidian canvas with precision developer-grade ergonomics, it rejects childish skeuomorphism and muddy dark surfaces in favor of a crisp, luminous workstation aesthetic.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Architectural Rigor:** Every viewport, panel, coordinate display, and schematic card signals structural integrity and mathematical precision.
- **Electric Focus:** Ultra-dark, layered slate backgrounds absorb visual noise, allowing vibrant electric cyan and energetic indigo signals to direct user momentum.
- **Craftsman Empowerment:** The UI feels like an advanced CAD environment rebuilt for voxel geometry—tactile, responsive, and unmistakably modern.

### Design Movement: Dark-Mode Technical Glassmorphism
The aesthetic synthesizes technical minimalism with high-end glassmorphism:
- **Obsidian Foundations:** Pure obsidian and deep midnight slates create tiered spatial depth without washed-out grays.
- **Chiseled Luminance:** Sub-pixel borders with subtle cyan-indigo specular highlights define card perimeters.
- **Voxel Grid Accents:** Subtle hairline grids and coordinate ticks reinforce the precision building motif while maintaining professional clarity.

## Colors

The color architecture is calibrated for long sessions in low-light environments, prioritizing optical contrast, clear tiering, and luminous focal states.

### Primary, Secondary & Accent Roles
- **Primary Electric Cyan (`#38BDF8`):** The primary beacon for interactive execution, active tool triggers, coordinate cursors, and glowing focal rings. It offers immediate optical vibration against deep dark grounds.
- **Secondary Indigo (`#6366F1`):** Powers key functional structures, prominent category tabs, active utility toggles, and base brand energy.
- **Tertiary Soft Indigo (`#818CF8`):** Serves as an atmospheric hover state, directional glyph fill, and subtle card boundary glow.

### Obsidian Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F19`):** The foundational infinite slate plane. All tool pages, canvas viewports, and main directories anchor here.
- **Card Surface (`#131C2E`):** Distinct mid-tier slate container with 1px structural framing, establishing primary containment for tool cards, palettes, and inspectors.
- **Elevated Surface (`#182234`):** Layered workspaces, hovering tooltips, active popovers, and elevated modal panels.
- **Structural Overlay (`#1E293B`):** Slotted docks, segmented control backgrounds, and depressed input bays.
